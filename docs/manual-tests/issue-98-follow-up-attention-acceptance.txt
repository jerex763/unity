Issue #98 — Follow-up attention and escalation manual acceptance

Use fictional data only. Put a unique RUN_ID in every fictional person's name.
Evaluate every date in the test church's configured timezone (default
Australia/Sydney). Do not use real contact details or sensitive interaction text.

Setup
1. Sign in as Admin/Pastor and create fictional follow-ups covering: new and
   unassigned for 23h/24h; assigned due yesterday/today/tomorrow; in_progress
   with last interaction just under/at 3 days; and due three days overdue.
2. Create two ordinary Leader accounts, one Member account, and an Other Church
   with one follow-up. Record IDs for cleanup.

Attention flags and ordering
3. Open Follow-up queue. Confirm distinct labels for Overdue, Due today, Stale,
   Unassigned 24h+, No action recorded, and Escalated, plus an obvious next action.
4. Confirm the 24-hour and 3-day boundaries trigger exactly at their threshold.
5. Confirm overdue rows sort ahead of due today, future, and no-date rows.
6. Confirm a due date three church-local calendar days overdue is Escalated.

Postponement
7. Move a future due date later for the first time: no reason is required and an
   auditable schedule-history row is created.
8. Move it later again: saving is blocked until choosing a safe operational
   reason or a qualifying linked interaction. Confirm no interaction summary is
   copied into schedule history.
9. Move an already-overdue date later: the same condition applies on its first
   postponement. Confirm earlier moves do not require a reason.
10. Attempt to reference an interaction from another follow-up and another
    church: both are rejected. A new linked interaction created after the current
    schedule was established is accepted.
11. After two later-date schedule changes, confirm Escalated appears.

Role and tenant boundaries
12. Admin/Pastor dashboard shows same-church unassigned-too-long and escalated
    work plus their own assigned open work.
13. Leader sees only their own assigned authorized work. Member is denied and
    the Other Church follow-up is absent/404.
14. Repeat the queue/editor checks at desktop and narrow mobile width; labels,
    next action, validation, reason selector, and Save remain readable/reachable.

Cleanup
15. Delete all mutable fictional rows for RUN_ID in dependency order, retain
    append-only audit data according to policy, and verify RUN_ID searches return
    no remaining mutable Person, Follow-up, Interaction, Membership, or User rows.

Audit/lifecycle note
- Follow-up due-date history cannot be added, changed, or deleted through the
  API or Django Admin. Follow-up workflow fields are also read-only in Admin so
  supported changes pass through the locked, audited API path.
- Due-date history deliberately cascades only when its parent Follow-up is
  removed. This preserves the authorized Person hard-delete contract, where the
  operational shell/history is deleted and only the separate safe AuditEvent is
  retained.
